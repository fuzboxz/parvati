# Factory Presets — Attribution

The factory patches shipped with Parvati (`presets/Factory/*.PRO` and
`presets/FactoryMulti/*.MUL`) are the **Ambika "goldencard" factory banks** by
**Emilie Gillet / Mutable Instruments**, taken from the Ambika firmware tree
(`controller/data/goldencard/`).
